About

Hey, I'm Olivier. Still a car guy at heart.

I started out designing wraps for performance cars, then moved into building the software behind them. I joined xix3D as employee #9 and its first designer, and grew from UX designer to product manager on Zeno, working with 3M and Ford.

Until July 2026 I made carbon fibre parts at Multimatic, and in parallel built a carbon fibre sword with a programmed LED blade at home. I build SetSelekta on the side. I also co-founded Ozan Productions, a Toronto photo and video studio.

At Multimatic I saw the last stage before a product reaches the customer - the same place I'd started in wrap shops. It changed who I count as a user: the people on the floor running the process matter as much as whoever buys the result. And I got to see, at scale, the kind of systems I'd been designing on paper for the Ford program actually run.

I approach everything problem-first. Mapping a user flow or paneling a Mustang, the goal is the same: the simplest solution that holds up in the real world.

Whatever I'm building, I stick around to see it finished.

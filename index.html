Building Zeno

xix3D is a small startup building Zeno, a 3D visualization and configuration platform for the automotive customization industry. Wrap shops, dealerships and enterprise partners use it to let customers design, preview and order vehicle customizations in real time.

I joined as employee #9 and the first designer of any kind, and was promoted to product manager a year in.

I owned Print & Cut, the export pipeline that took designs from the screen to the plotter. I tested it in real production software at a wrap shop, and the first file out became a real wrap job.

Inside the factory floor

I manufactured structural carbon fibre components for low-volume, high-performance vehicles. I took the role on purpose: after years of building software for people who make physical things, I wanted to work inside the environments that software is supposed to serve.

In parallel: the sword

At home I built a carbon fibre sword from scratch, with a programmable LED blade inside. It taught me mould making, composite layup and AI-assisted programming, end to end. ~40 hours over 9 months - a three-layer carbon wet layup in low-volume composite materials, and 144 programmed LEDs.

Built at home, on my own time, with my own materials. No Multimatic tooling, processes, parts or IP.

Plan your festival with friends

SetSelekta is a website that helps groups coordinate at music festivals and events where performances overlap. Everyone picks the sets they want to see, and when two sets clash, it builds one plan the whole group can follow.

I made it for my friends and me to plan EDC Orlando and Ultra Miami, then decided to make it usable by anyone.

I design, build, deploy and fix it alone, live at setselekta.com.
